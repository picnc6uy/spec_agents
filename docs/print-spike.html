Print spike — what to check

  1. Print from Chrome or Edge. Scale 100%. Do not tick "fit to page". Leave margins on Default.
  2. Measure the ruler: it must be 100 mm. If it is not, note the measured length (this becomes the scale correction).
  3. Measure one card: 63 x 88 mm, within 0.5 mm.
  4. Measure from the paper's top-left corner to the corner mark: it should be 10 mm each way. The difference is the printer's feed offset.
  5. Cut card 5 and check it against a real poker card or sleeve.
